What happens in that dark frame:
  • The header logo you upload for dark mode replaces the standard one (the wording here is the placeholder wordmark).
  • Colours come from the new dark palette in Trade Portal Options → Dark Mode; every value there is editable.
  • The switch icon flips from a moon to a sun, and stays where your other header actions are.
  • The logo is now 56px tall by default (was 48px) and can be set up to 160px; the header grows to fit it.
